# Welcome to cosm

Your notes, and an AI that reads and writes them. The chat is on the right.

**Every day**
- /recall — where things stand, and what to pick up
- drop anything in 01-inbox/, then /inbox files it
- "go through my email"
- "keep my expenses in a table": the AI keeps tables for you and adds them up
- /reflect when you're done, /log for a record

**Weekly:** /review · **New version:** /update

**Keys when it asks:** ==y== yes · ==n== no · ==s== skip · ==1== ==2== … pick · ==a== archive · ==d== draft

**First time:** type /setup in the chat. It takes about 3 minutes.

Your notes are plain files in this folder. Open any of them, and change what's wrong.

New to writing notes here? [[HELP|A one-minute guide]].
